ORBITIQ is an independent engineering prototype exploring how AI can help manage satellite-to-cellular connectivity.
ORBITIQ starts with publicly available cellular infrastructure data rather than fabricated network topology.
It combines this with public orbital data to determine satellite visibility and candidate connectivity paths.
The digital twin then models thousands of mobile devices and network conditions.
When a satellite becomes congested, the system detects abnormal behavior and evaluates the potential impact.
Machine-learning models predict which users are likely to experience degraded connectivity.
The optimization engine ranks alternative satellite paths and explains why a handoff is recommended.
Engineers can simulate an outage and compare network behavior before and after the AI recommendation.
A grounded AI copilot lets engineers investigate incidents using telemetry and model results.
Real data, AI and ML, digital twin, network optimization, GenAI. ORBITIQ. Independent engineering research prototype. No proprietary SpaceX data used.
